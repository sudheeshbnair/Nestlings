About Us

Nestlings Premium Preschool

is the vision of passionate educator, Hridya R Krishna, who believe that every child deserves a joyful and nurturing start to their learning journey.

Hridya R Krishna, founder of Nestlings Premium Preschool, has a unique blend of corporate and academic experience. With two years of professional experience at UST Global and five years of teaching experience, she combines organizational skills with her Montessori background to create a structured yet child-centered learning environment.

Hridya is deeply committed to nurturing young minds with care, creativity, and a Montessori-inspired approach. Their shared passion for teaching and belief in holistic development makes Nestlings Premium Preschool a warm and inspiring place where children can grow, explore, and flourish.
