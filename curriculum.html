Curriculum Overview

Our approach ensures holistic development - balancing academics, social skills, creativity and physical growth in a joyful, stress-free environment. It introduces foundational literacy, numeracy, scientific exploration and creative arts while also teaching essential skills like sharing, collaboration and following instructions, preparing children for future academic and personal success. We encourage creativity and make children become independent learners.
