Toddlers Curriculum (1-2 Years)

1. Physical Development

Gross Motor Skills
Walking, running, climbing, jumping, balancing
Riding push toys, ball play (throwing, rolling, kicking)
Outdoor play for strength and coordination
Fine Motor Skills
Stacking blocks, puzzles with knobs
Scribbling with crayons, finger painting
Self-feeding, turning pages, simple dressing (zipping, buttoning with help)
2. Language & Communication

Expanding vocabulary through songs, rhymes, and stories
Naming objects, animals, body parts, colors
Encouraging two to three-word sentences
Listening and responding to simple instructions
Puppet play, storytelling, and circle-time conversations
3. Cognitive Development

Identifying shapes, colors, and basic numbers (1-5)
Sorting and matching objects
Cause-and-effect play (push-pull toys, stacking, nesting cups)
Simple puzzles and problem-solving activities
Exploring nature and asking "what/why" questions
4. Socio-Emotional Development

Parallel play moving toward cooperative play
Learning to share and take turns with guidance
Developing routines (snack time, clean-up time)
Expressing feelings and recognizing emotions in self/others
Building independence (washing hands, tidying toys)
5. Creative & Sensory Development

Music, dance, and movement activities
Finger painting, clay play, sponge painting
Dramatic play: dolls, kitchen sets, dress-up corner
Sensory bins (water, sand, rice always supervised)
Rhythm instrument and simple action songs
6. Environmental Awareness

Identifying animals, birds, plants, and insects
Learning about family members and community helpers
Observing seasonal changes, weather, and nature walks
Simple eco-habits (watering plants, not littering)
Celebrating cultural events and festivals in simple ways
7. Daily Routine

Circle time: greeting, weather, songs, calendar talk
Free play: blocks, pretend play, outdoor play
Structured activities: storytelling, art, language/numbers
Snack & rest time: self-feeding and quiet time
At this stage, children learn best through play, sensory exploration, and routines.
